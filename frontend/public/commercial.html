How Tarbiyah Live Works
Every Minute Counts in Our Classroom
While one student recites, every other child stays active through our unique 3-step system. Here's exactly what happens:
1
While a classmate recites...

Your Child Practices Independently

Your child opens the built-in Quran reader and practices their own assigned ayah — at their own pace, on their own surah. One child could be on Surah Al-Baqarah while another is on Para 15.

💡 This means no child ever "falls behind" — each one works at their own level, regardless of where others are.
2
At the same time...

They Observe & Answer Scholar Prompts

The scholar asks everyone watching: "Did your classmate recite correctly?" Your child taps Yes or No. The scholar then reveals the correct answer.

Kids who answered correctly earn XP — actual points that go into their profile and the class leaderboard.

💡 This is the key difference. Instead of zoning out, your child is actively listening, judging Tajweed, and getting rewarded for paying attention — even when it's not their turn.
3
When it's their turn...

They Recite Live with the Scholar

The scholar selects your child. They recite directly. The scholar scores their Tajweed in real-time — and the score immediately updates on the class leaderboard.

💡 Every child gets their spotlight moment. Scoring is transparent — your child knows exactly where they stand.

Common Questions
Everything Parents Ask Us

What age group is this for?

Tarbiyah Live is designed for children aged 6-14. The scholar adapts the session complexity based on the group level.

My child is a complete beginner. Can they join?

Absolutely. Each child reads their own assigned ayah at their own pace. A beginner on Surah Al-Fatiha sits alongside someone on Para 10 — they never feel behind because they're working on their own level.

How many kids are in each class?

5-8 children per batch. Small enough for personal scholar attention, large enough for healthy leaderboard competition.

Who are the scholars?

Certified Quran scholars with ijazah (formal authorization to teach). Every scholar is vetted and trained in our interactive classroom methodology.

How often are the classes?

16 live sessions per month — roughly 4 per week. Each session is scheduled at a fixed time for your batch so it becomes routine.

What if my child misses a class?

The Journey Map marks it as "Missed" and moves forward. Your child doesn't lose their place — they continue from where they are in the next session.

Can I track what my child is doing?

Yes. The parent dashboard shows XP earned, classes attended vs missed, streak days, leaderboard position, and weekly activity charts. Full transparency.

What device do they need?

Any smartphone, tablet, or computer with a browser. No app download required — it works directly in the browser at imam.vercel.app.

Is there a free trial?

We're currently enrolling first batches. Contact us for a demo session to see exactly how the classroom works before committing.
